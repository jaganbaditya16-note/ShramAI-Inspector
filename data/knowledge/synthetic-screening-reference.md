# What a complete payslip contains (generic screening guidance)

A payroll or wage document typically evidences: employee identifier, pay period,
basic wage or salary, allowances (for example house rent or conveyance), gross
earnings, deductions (for example provident fund or professional tax), and net
pay. When gross or net components are absent, the screening pipeline raises a
signal so an inspector can verify the full payroll record manually.

# What attendance evidence contains (generic screening guidance)

An attendance register typically evidences: worker identifiers, dated entries,
present/absent or half-day markers, working hours or shifts, and overtime where
applicable. When none of these markers appear in an attendance-style document,
the pipeline raises a signal for manual review.
